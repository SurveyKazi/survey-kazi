SurveyKazi

About SurveyKazi

SurveyKazi is a survey platform designed to connect people with online surveys and reward them with points for participating.

Our goal is to make sharing opinions simple, accessible and rewarding.

How It Works

1. Create an account.

2. Find available surveys.

3. Complete surveys and earn points.

4. Use your available points to request rewards.

Our Mission

We aim to build a simple platform where users can participate in research and share their opinions while receiving rewards for eligible survey participation.

Important Information

Survey availability and rewards may vary. The current SurveyKazi website is a project version, and payment, account verification and other backend services will be connected before the platform is used for real transactions.
